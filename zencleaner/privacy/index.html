Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Zen Cleaner ("the App", "we", "us") is a photo, video and contact cleaning app for iPhone. This Privacy Policy explains what the App does — and, more importantly, does not do — with your information.

The short version: your photos, videos and contacts never leave your iPhone. All the cleaning happens locally on your device. We have no servers of our own and no account system. Apart from handling subscriptions (section 3), the only thing the App sends out is a small set of anonymous usage statistics — for example "a scan finished" or "40 screenshots deleted" — that are not tied to your identity (section 4). We never see your photos, videos or contacts.

1. We do not collect personal data

All photo, video and contact processing happens on your device. We do not operate a backend, we do not require an account, and we do not transmit your content or a profile of you to us or to any third party.

Specifically, we do not collect, store on our servers, sell, rent or share:

  • your photos, videos or screenshots;
  • your contacts;
  • your name, email address, phone number or contact details;
  • your location (the App never asks for location access);
  • device identifiers used for advertising or tracking.

What the App does send — the data needed to run subscriptions and anonymous usage statistics — is described in sections 3 and 4.

2. How the App uses on-device data

To do its job, the App needs to read certain information on your device. This information is processed locally and is never uploaded to us.

Photos and videos

With your permission, the App reads your photo library to find similar or duplicate photos, blurred shots, screenshots, screen recordings and large videos, and to help you free up space. Image analysis (finding look-alikes and choosing the best shot in a group) is performed on-device using Apple's Vision framework. When you choose to delete items, the deletion is carried out by iOS through the system photo library; deleted items go to the Recently Deleted album and can be restored there for 30 days before iOS removes them permanently.

You can grant full or limited photo access, or deny access entirely, from the iOS Settings app at any time. The App only reads what iOS allows it to see.

Contacts

With your permission, the App reads your contacts to find duplicate and empty entries and to help you merge duplicates into a single contact. All of this happens on-device. Merging and deleting contacts is performed through the system contacts store on your explicit action. The optional backup feature exports your contacts to a standard .vcf file that you control and share through the iOS share sheet — the file is never sent to us.

You can revoke contacts access at any time from the iOS Settings app.

3. Subscriptions and payments

Zen Cleaner offers an optional auto-renewing subscription (Zen Cleaner Pro). All purchases are processed by Apple through In-App Purchase. We never see or receive your credit card number, billing address or Apple ID password.

Payment, billing and renewal are handled entirely by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy and the terms of your Apple Account. The App only learns whether an active entitlement exists so it can unlock Pro features. You can view or cancel your subscription at any time in Settings → Apple Account → Subscriptions on your device.

To check whether a subscription is active, the App uses RevenueCat, Inc., a subscription service acting on our behalf. RevenueCat receives the purchase record issued by Apple, a random anonymous identifier and basic technical details (such as the App and iOS version) — never your name, email address or payment details. RevenueCat handles this data under its own Privacy Policy.

4. Anonymous usage analytics

To understand how the App is used and to improve it, the App sends a small set of anonymous usage events to Amplitude, Inc., an analytics provider acting on our behalf. Events describe product moments — the App was opened, an onboarding page was viewed, a scan finished, the paywall was shown, a trial or subscription was started, a cleaning action was performed — together with counts, categories, sizes in megabytes and yes/no flags (for example: "12 similar groups found", "40 screenshots deleted, 80 MB"). Each event also carries basic technical details: device model, iOS version, language, the country set in your iPhone's settings, the App version and, when we are comparing two versions of a screen, which one you were shown.

These events never contain your content: no photos, videos, screenshots, contact names, phone numbers, emails, file names or any text from your device. They are not linked to your identity: the App uses a random installation identifier generated on your device (it is deleted with the App and is not the Apple advertising identifier or vendor identifier). The App is set up so that Amplitude does not record your IP address, city-level location or mobile carrier, and there is no account information to send. The data is not used for advertising and is not shared with ad networks. Amplitude processes this data in the United States under its own Privacy Policy.

5. No ad tracking, no advertising SDKs

The App contains no advertising SDKs and does not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA). We do not build advertising profiles and we do not participate in cross-app or cross-site tracking. The anonymous analytics described above stays within Zen Cleaner: it is not combined with data from other apps or websites.

6. Children

Zen Cleaner is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store your personal data, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on our side. The anonymous usage events are not tied to you — we have no way to tell which of them came from your device — so we cannot look them up, correct or delete them on request. Uninstalling the App stops the collection and erases the random identifier. You remain in full control of your content through the iOS Photos and Contacts apps and your device settings, and you can uninstall the App at any time.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
